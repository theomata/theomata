## About

I'm interested in understanding how systems and networks work, how they fail, and how they can be monitored and improved. I use practical projects and lab environments to explore technical concepts, troubleshoot issues, and document what I learn.

- Building and documenting cybersecurity and networking labs.
- Exploring network services, logging, and security monitoring.
- Practicing in controlled environments and documenting the process.
- Learning continuously through hands-on projects and technical study.
